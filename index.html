Data Science Philosophy
01
🔍
Evidence Before the Model
Every column I keep, drop, or transform gets checked first - missingness, seasonality, collinearity - the evidence comes before the decision, not after.
02
📏
Beat a Real Baseline
A model is only as good as what it's compared against. I backtest against naive and seasonal baselines, not just a train/test split, and I report the worst case too.
03
⚙️
Production-Minded Pipelines
Good analytics needs reliable data paths. I design pipelines from ingestion through features, validation, deployment, and monitoring - not just a one-off notebook.
04
🤝
Insight Delivery
The best analysis still has to land with humans. I translate models and numbers into clear workflows for operators, leaders, and product teams.
